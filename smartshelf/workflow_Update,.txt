You are a senior Product Designer, UX Engineer, and Next.js developer working on the SmartShelf pharmacy inventory management system.

IMPORTANT

Before making any changes, analyze the existing SmartShelf project and preserve the current architecture.

Do NOT redesign the application.

Do NOT change authentication.

Do NOT change user roles.

Do NOT change reports.

Do NOT change AI.

Do NOT change offline functionality.

The objective of this update is to optimize the pharmacy sales workflow based on real-world observations inside community pharmacies in Sierra Leone.

======================================================
BACKGROUND
======================================================

During field observation, pharmacists were found using a physical calculator to calculate the customer's total before recording the sale.

Current workflow:

Customer requests medicine

↓

Pharmacist uses calculator

↓

Calculates total manually

↓

Receives payment

↓

Records sale inside SmartShelf

↓

Inventory updates

This means pharmacists are using TWO systems simultaneously:

1. Calculator
2. SmartShelf

This creates unnecessary work and slows down customer service during busy periods.

The goal of this update is to make SmartShelf itself become the calculator.

The pharmacist should never need a separate calculator while recording a sale.

======================================================
NEW SALES EXPERIENCE
======================================================

Every medicine already has a stored selling price.

Never ask the pharmacist to manually enter the medicine price during a sale.

When a medicine is selected, SmartShelf should automatically know its selling price.

======================================================
BIG 5 QUICK SALE SCREEN
======================================================

The existing quick sale interface should become a lightweight Point of Sale.

Each medicine tile should display:

Medicine Name

Selling Price

Current Quantity Selected

Large Plus button

Large Minus button

Example

+---------------------------+

Paracetamol

SLE 15

[-] 2 [+]

Subtotal: SLE 30

+---------------------------+

Every tap on the + button increases quantity by one.

Every tap on the - button decreases quantity.

The subtotal updates instantly.

======================================================
RUNNING TOTAL
======================================================

Display a sticky summary at the bottom of the screen.

Example

--------------------------------

Items

3

Total

SLE 75

--------------------------------

This total must update immediately after every quantity change.

No refresh.

No save button.

When the pharmacist presses

Do not change the existing inventory logic.

======================================================

Preserve that functionality.

Large –

Update:

Grand Total

======================================================
MEDICINE PRICES
======================================================

Medicine selling prices must always come from the database.

The pharmacist should never type prices during checkout.

Changing a medicine's selling price in Inventory should automatically affect future sales.

Existing sales must preserve the historical selling price used during that transaction.

======================================================
CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE
======================================================

The goal is to serve customers faster.

A pharmacist should be able to:

Tap medicine

Increase quantity

See total

Receive payment

Record sale

in only a few seconds.

======================================================
UI REQUIREMENTS
======================================================

Make buttons large enough for touch screens.

Optimize for tablets.

Optimize for low computer literacy.

Avoid excessive typing.

Minimize clicks.
